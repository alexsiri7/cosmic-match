Privacy Policy — Cosmic Match

Last updated: October 2026

Cosmic Match is a puzzle game. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

Your game scores and progress are stored locally on your device in an encrypted database. They are not uploaded as game data. A screenshot you attach to feedback may show the current score on screen.

Personal data

No account is required and the app does not read contacts, location, advertising identifiers or other device identifiers. The app does not collect personal data on its own; any personal data in feedback you submit is data you chose to include.

Crash reporting

Release builds send crash reports and app-start/stop session events to Sentry.io. These include a stack trace, app version, device model, OS version and a random per-install identifier so Sentry can count how many installs an error affects. No name, email, account, IP address or advertising ID is stored. Sentry retains this data for 90 days.

Feedback (optional)

If you use the in-app feedback feature and tick the consent box, the feedback type, your description, the annotated screenshot, app version, OS and device model are sent to a Cloudflare Worker operated by the developer, which files them as an issue on the public GitHub repository github.com/alexsiri7/cosmic-match. Public issues can be read by anyone and indexed by search engines, and are kept until deleted. Please do not include personal information in feedback. If a submission cannot be delivered immediately (for example offline), it is held in an encrypted local queue, retried when connectivity returns, and deleted after 7 days.

Internet permission

The app requests internet access for crash reporting, feedback submission, checking Google Play for app updates, and downloading the app font from Google Fonts on first launch. Gameplay works offline.

Third-party services

  • Sentry.io — crash reporting. Sentry privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Workers — transport for feedback submissions. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • GitHub, Inc. — storage of submitted feedback as public issues. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Play / Google Fonts — update checks and font download. Google privacy policy.

Data deletion and your rights

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data. Unsent feedback expires after 7 days and can be deleted at any time with the "Clear feedback queue" button on the home screen. To have a submitted feedback issue (including its screenshot) deleted, or to ask what was stored, contact the developer at the address below quoting the issue number shown after submission; the issue will be closed and deleted and the screenshot purged.
